# How to Work Through Assignments

## Daily loop (3-4 hours)
1. **Learn (~1 h):** read only the "Learn first" links in the assignment.
2. **Build (~2 h):** do the tasks, easiest to hardest, in `week-XX/code/`.
3. **Prove (~30 min):** screenshots into `screenshots/`, fill in the write-up.
4. **Check in:** explain what you built in your own words; answer 2-3 probing questions.

## Rules
- You write the code. AI explains concepts, asks questions and reviews; it does not write your solution.
- `TRADEOFFS.md` and "What I built" are always in your own words.
- Never commit secrets (API keys, X archive, output CSVs). Use `.gitignore` and `config.example.json`.
- Commit at least once per task, with a meaningful message.

## Evidence
- Screenshots should show your code/terminal and your name or username.
- Name them `a1-task2.png` etc. and place in the week's `screenshots/` folder.

## "Done" for an assignment
- [ ] Runs from a clean clone with one documented command
- [ ] Tests exist (from A6 onward)
- [ ] No secrets committed
- [ ] Screenshots added
- [ ] "What I built" written in your own words
- [ ] Progress table in `README.md` updated

## Write-up (optional post)
Short post: what you built, one thing that broke, what you learned, one screenshot.
